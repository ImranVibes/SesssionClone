Privacy Policy

            100% Private
          

          
        

        

          

            Your Data Stays on Your Device
            SessionClone does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, cookies, or storage keys. There is no remote backend, no user tracking, and no external analytics.
          


          
1. What SessionClone Accesses

          
• Cookies: Read and written only for the website you actively target when opening the extension or using clone, paste, export, or import.

          
• Site Storage: LocalStorage and SessionStorage are accessed directly in your active tab via standard browser scripting.

          
• Clipboard: Accessed strictly when you click "Paste" or "Copy".

          
• Vault Profiles & Settings: Stored locally in your browser's private extension storage.


          
2. Data Storage & Encryption

          
All settings and Vault profiles persist on your local device only. For sensitive exports, optional military-grade AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation is available.


          
3. Permissions & Zero Remote Communication

          
The extension requests only permissions necessary to clone and manage sessions across the sites you visit. No network telemetry or browsing history is ever transmitted.
